## 2024-10-31 - Cache Intl.DateTimeFormat in Hot Paths
**Learning:** `Intl.DateTimeFormat` instantiation carries a massive performance penalty (taking roughly ~40-60x longer than just formatting an already-instantiated instance, blocking the UI thread for multiple milliseconds per call). When used inside tight loops or frequent render cycles like `campHour` (which is used in a 5-minute sampling loop) and chat message rendering, this creates a severe architectural bottleneck.
**Action:** Always cache and reuse top-level or lazily initialized `Intl.DateTimeFormat` closures instead of instantiating them on the fly in synchronous functions. Always wrap lazy initializations inside `try...catch` blocks to preserve fallback behavior and avoid script-load-time errors in unsupported environments.
