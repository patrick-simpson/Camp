## 2024-05-18 - Intl.DateTimeFormat instantiation in frequently called functions
**Learning:** `Intl.DateTimeFormat` instantiation is expensive (takes ~4 seconds for 10000 iterations vs 70ms when cached). In functions called frequently (like `campNow()`, `formatEasternStamp()`), instantiating it inside the function causes significant performance bottlenecks. Memory explicitly calls out: "Avoid instantiating `Intl.DateTimeFormat` inside synchronous render loops or frequently called functions. Cache and reuse top-level formatter instances or lazily-instantiated closures instead to prevent performance bottlenecks. Ensure lazy initializations of such APIs remain inside `try...catch` blocks to preserve fallback behavior and avoid script-load-time `RangeError`s in unsupported environments."
**Action:** Extract `Intl.DateTimeFormat` instantiations to lazily-evaluated module-scoped variables wrapped in a closure or lazily instantiated pattern, to avoid the initialization overhead on every call.
